oxvox: Play Store upload and publish guide

Do not publish until every OPEN item in PLAY_UPLOAD_NOTES.md is resolved. The Oxford word lists, the listening audio, and the short stories need a license or written permission.

1. Before you start

  • Google Play Console developer account ($25 one-time fee). Create it at play.google.com/console.
  • Your identity verification and developer profile completed in Play Console.
  • Backup of android/upload-keystore.jks and android/key.properties. Losing them means you can't update the app.
  • Privacy policy live: https://nazmul-hussain.github.io/oxvox-cdn/privacy.html

2. Build the release file

Bump the version in pubspec.yaml first (current 1.0.0+1 is fine for the first upload). Then run:

cd oxvox-flutter
flutter clean
flutter pub get
flutter build appbundle --release

Upload this file: build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab

Check it's signed with your upload key: if android/key.properties is missing, the build falls back to a debug key, and Play will reject it.

3. Create the app in Play Console

  1. Open All apps → Create app.
  2. App name: oxvox. Default language. App or game: App. Free or paid: Free.
  3. Accept the declarations (developer policies, US export laws).

5. Policy sections (left menu, under Policy)

  1. App content → Privacy policy: paste the privacy policy URL above.
  2. App content → Ads: the app has no ads. Say No (confirm before submitting).
  3. App content → App access: the app works without login. Say All functionality is available without special access. Add a test account only if you choose to.
  4. App content → Content rating: complete the questionnaire. Educational app, no violence, no user-generated content.
  5. App content → Target audience: choose the age groups. Don't pick under-13 unless you meet the children's policy rules.
  6. App content → Data safety: declare account data (name, username/email/phone, password) and learning progress. Data is encrypted in transit. Users can delete their account in the app.
  7. App content → News apps / COVID / Government / Financial: answer No where it applies.

6. Closed testing (required for new personal developer accounts)

Accounts created after November 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days before production access. Check the current requirement in Play Console when you start.

  1. Open Testing → Closed testing → create a track.
  2. Upload app-release.aab, add release notes, and roll out.
  3. Add tester emails (a Google Group or list) and share the opt-in link.
  4. Wait for the 14-day period, then apply for production access if Play Console asks.

7. Production release

  1. Open Production → Create new release.
  2. Upload app-release.aab. Add release notes.
  3. Review the warnings Play shows (fix any errors; warnings are usually OK).
  4. Click Review release → Start rollout to production.
  5. Review usually takes a few hours to a few days. Check your email and the Policy status page.

8. After publishing

  • To update the app: bump version: in pubspec.yaml (the +N must increase), rebuild the .aab, and upload a new release.
  • Keep the keystore backed up.
